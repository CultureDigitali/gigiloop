# Product Hunt — launch copy

**Product name:** GigiLoop
**Tagline:** The autonomous coding skill that won't ship until its own work is backed by evidence.
**Topics:** Developer Tools, Open Source, Artificial Intelligence, Productivity

**First comment (maker comment):**
GigiLoop is an OpenCode skill for the moment when "keep trying until it works" isn't enough.
It establishes a baseline, defines measurable acceptance criteria, iterates on the highest-impact
issue, adversarially reviews its own diff, reconciles scores after critique, and only ships after
a final verification gate.

Why it's not just another loop:
- Evidence-gated scoring — no proof, no score.
- Score reconciliation — a confirmed flaw can lower the score.
- Evidence-gated red-team — real findings only, never fabricated.
- All-criteria gate + plateau→re-strategy + honest exit.

It ships a reproducible benchmark protocol (one-pass vs naive-loop vs GigiLoop, same commit,
hidden evaluators). MIT licensed.
